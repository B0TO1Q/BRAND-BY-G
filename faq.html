Cost & ownership

What does a website cost?

A website build is $2,500 for up to 3 pages, and each additional page is $500.

The care plan is optional, at $75 a month. The Be Found package for Google search and maps (town pages, blog posts and your Google Business Profile) starts at $1,500, one-time, and monthly content is $300 a month for 2 new blog posts or town pages. Add-ons are priced per item: $150 per extra blog post or town page, $150 for an email signature design, $350 and up per logo refresh. Every price is listed on the Pricing page.

Who owns the website?

You do. When the site is done, it's handed off to you clean: the files, the domain and the accounts in your name. You're never locked in to me.

Are there other costs besides your fee?

The main one is your domain name (your .com), which you pay for yourself each year and which stays in your name. A site built the way I build them can be hosted for free or close to it, and I'll walk you through your options before we start.

Do you charge monthly?

Only if you want the care plan. The build is a one-time price.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. We agree on the price and what's included in writing before any work starts, you pay for the build, and that's it. The care plan, if you want it, is month to month.

What if I want to leave?

You take everything with you: the site, the domain and the accounts are already in your name. There's no buyout fee, and I'll help you hand things off to whoever comes next.

The build

What do you need from me to get started?
  • Your logo, if you have one
  • Photos of your work, your crew or your space — phone photos are fine
  • The services you offer and the towns you cover
  • How customers should reach you: phone, text, email
  • Any reviews you're proud of

Don't have all of it? That's normal. We'll figure it out together.

How long does it take?

It depends on how many pages you need and how quickly the photos and details come in. I'll give you a target date before we start, and you'll see a real draft on your own phone along the way.

Will I see it before it goes live?

Yes. You'll get a working draft you can click through on your own phone, and we adjust it together before anything goes live.

Will it work on phones?

Every site is designed for the phone first, since that's where most of your customers will find you, then for tablets and computers. See the Work section for examples, with a Phone view on each.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. I can rebuild it, carry over what's worth keeping, and keep your existing domain so people still find you.

After launch

What does the care plan cover?

Up to 4 small updates a month, done within 2 business days. One update is one change: new photos, changed hours or prices, a new or retired service, a seasonal note, or an update to your Google Business Profile.

Unused updates don't roll over. Anything past 4 in a month, and anything bigger like a new page, a new section or a redesign, is quoted before I start, so there are no surprise invoices.

What if I don't want the care plan?

That's fine. The site is yours either way. If you need a change later, just ask and I'll quote it.

Can I cancel the care plan?

Yes, any time. It's month to month, and your site keeps running either way.

Can I edit the site myself?

It's yours, so yes. The sites are hand-built, which keeps them fast, but that also means edits mean changing code. Most owners find it easier to send me a text and let the care plan handle it.

Will I show up on Google?

Every site is built with the basics Google looks for: clear page titles, your business details, a sitemap, and previews that look good when your link is shared. Sites are built to load fast too, which Google pays attention to: the Hafele Landscaping site scores 97+ on Google PageSpeed on both phones and computers.

Nobody can honestly guarantee you a #1 spot on Google, so I won't. If you want more, the Be Found package (from $1,500, one-time) adds town pages, blog posts and a properly set-up Google Business Profile, and monthly content ($300 a month) keeps it growing, month to month. Paid ads aren't part of my offer; I'll point you to someone good.
