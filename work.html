Client build · Landscaping & snow removal

Hafele Landscaping

Draft with the ownerVisit the live site

A site that knows what season it is — and an estimate in two taps, from the driveway.

Hafele is a Burlington, CT crew covering lawn and turf care, grounds maintenance, lawn renovation, gutters and snow removal across fifteen towns in Hartford and Litchfield counties. The brief: classic, professional, built to last — and turn visits into estimate requests.

  • A site that changes with the seasons. It reads today's date and switches to spring, summer, fall or winter, with a season tone on the hero photo and quote options for that time of year, like Fall Cleanup or Reserve Snow Removal. Visitors can flip between seasons with tabs.
  • Four-Season Care. An annual agreement for year-round upkeep, with its own band on Home and Services, a request-a-walkthrough button, and an option in every season.
  • Two-step estimate request. Pick a service and a time window, then name, cell and address. Send opens a text to the owner with everything filled in, with an email fallback.
  • Call, Text and Get an Estimate pinned to the bottom of the screen on phones.
  • Trust up front: a Licensed & Insured · BBB Accredited · 5.0 on Google line under the headline, reviews in customers' own words on the home page, and an About page written with owners Nate & Megan.
  • A blog of seasonal how-tos written for Connecticut homeowners, like when to overseed, fall lawn prep and what to look for in a snow removal contract, each ending with a way to book.
  • Service-area pages for Burlington, Avon, Farmington, Canton and Torrington, plus a hub covering all 15 towns, so people searching their own town find Hafele.
  • Built to be found: on its own domain, set up so Google can read the business details, and every page shares cleanly when it's texted or posted.
  • Small touches: real job photos and a walk-around video, sections that fade up as you scroll, smooth scrolling with a mouse or trackpad, and a Verse of the Day above the footer.

Spec concept · Wildlife photography

Dani Hamm

Spec conceptVisit the live site

A gallery that feels like being out there at first light.

A portfolio and store concept for a Sony wildlife photographer, built as a pitch. Dark, cinematic, quiet type, and the photos given the whole screen. Colour blocks stand in for her photos until she supplies her own.

  • Three full-screen scenes — first light, fog, the moment — that lead into the work.
  • Portfolio filtered by birds, mammals, landscapes and concerts.
  • Shop with eight products: prints, canvas, framed, metal, mugs, postcards, calendars and tees.
  • Product pages where the buyer picks the photo, size, colour and quantity, with a working cart.
  • About and contact pages with an inquiry form.
  • Small touches: gallery lighting on every image, drifting fog and light beams behind each page, and smooth, slightly slower scrolling with a mouse or trackpad.
