On a phone

Hafele is a Burlington, CT crew covering lawns, landscapes, hydroseeding, gutters and plowing across fifteen towns. The brief: classic, professional, built to last — and turn visits into quote requests.

  • Two-step quote request. Pick a service, a day and a time window (snow skips the day), then name, cell and address. Send opens a text to the owner with everything filled in, with an email fallback.
  • Call, Text and Get a quote pinned to the bottom of the screen on phones.
  • Three pages: Home, Services with a common-questions section, and About & Contact.
  • Real job photos from the crew, straightened and cropped, plus a walk-around video.
  • Small touches: a soft sun glow and ground fog on the hero, sections that fade up as you scroll, and a Verse of the Day strip above the footer.

A portfolio and store concept for a Sony wildlife photographer, built as a pitch. Dark, cinematic, quiet type, and the photos given the whole screen. Colour blocks stand in for her photos until she supplies her own.

  • Three full-screen scenes — first light, fog, the moment — that lead into the work.
  • Portfolio filtered by birds, mammals, landscapes and concerts.
  • Shop with eight products: prints, canvas, framed, metal, mugs, postcards, calendars and tees.
  • Product pages where the buyer picks the photo, size, colour and quantity, with a working cart.
  • About and contact pages with an inquiry form.
